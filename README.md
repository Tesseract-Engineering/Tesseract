## Tesseract

**What it is:** Tesseract is a "company brain with hands." It connects what your company already knows into one living map. It answers questions with that full context and can act on what it finds, like filing a ticket or looping in the right person, but only with your approval.

**Who it's for:** Teams that waste time rebuilding things that already exist, tracking down the source of truth, or sitting through meetings to learn why something was built a certain way. That includes engineering orgs, and also the people we interviewed in banking, compliance, and law. In each of those fields, the knowledge exists but nobody can reach it when they need it.

**How it works:**
1. **Ingest:** A fleet of agents fans out across your existing tools (GitHub, Notion, Slack, Jira, and more). Each agent knows how its source works and pulls out the important parts: services, owners, decisions, and dependencies.
2. **Link:** A linking layer finds where those pieces describe the same thing. For example, one service might show up in a pull request, a Slack thread, and a design doc. The linker merges them into a single node, joining the separate islands of knowledge into one connected graph.
3. **Answer and act:** You sign in with your own credentials, so you only see what you're allowed to see. You can ask things like "Can we shut down `/v1/legacy/export` next sprint?" or "How do I get production database access?" Tesseract answers using the full graph and suggests actions for you to approve.
